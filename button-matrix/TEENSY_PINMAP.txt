20-button board interface, revision 1

Board
  Matrix: 4 rows x 5 columns, buttons numbered 1..20, left-to-right then
  top-to-bottom. The 20-position D10 reference drawing is not available here;
  its two-row/14-pad connector cannot be assumed to match this 20-button design.
  J1 is a keyed 2x10 (20-position) right-angle IDC header. Pin 20 is omitted
  for the key.

J1 pinout (board view; even pins are the second IDC row)
  1  ROW0       2  +5V_LED
  3  ROW1       4  +5V_LED
  5  ROW2       6  GND
  7  ROW3       8  +5V_LED
  9  COL0      10  GND
 11  COL1      12  COL2
 13  COL3      14  +3V3
 15  COL4      16  LED_SIN
 17  LED_SCLK  18  LED_XLAT
 19  NC        20  KEY (no pin / no copper)

The board's LED driver is a TLC5947, powered from +3V3 and controlled by
SIN/SCLK/XLAT. Its open-drain constant-current outputs sink the 20 LED
cathodes; each LED anode is fed from +5V through its own 470 ohm resistor.
The driver IC, not 20 Teensy PWM pins, generates independent LED brightness.
The driver's BLANK, DCPRG and VPRG pins are hard-strapped for normal operation
per the selected TLC5947 datasheet circuit. Verify these straps against the
exact package before fabrication.

Teensy 4.1 pin assignments
  ROW0..ROW3: pins 0, 1, 2, 3
  ROW4:       pin 4
  COL0..COL4: pins 5, 6, 7, 8, 9 (INPUT_PULLUP)
  LED_SIN:    pin 11
  LED_SCLK:   pin 13
  LED_XLAT:   pin 10

All Teensy pins are 3.3 V only. Do not connect the +5V_LED supply to any
Teensy GPIO. J1 pin 14 supplies 3.3 V to the board's low-current logic only;
the 20 LED current comes from +5V_LED. Confirm the host's 3.3 V budget and
common-ground arrangement before connecting the board.

Matrix polarity
  One 1N4148 is in series with each switch, anode at its column and cathode at
  its row. The selected row is driven LOW and the other rows HIGH. A pressed
  switch therefore reads LOW at its column. Do not omit the diodes: they
  prevent multi-key ghosting.

Connector power notes
  Pins 2, 4 and 8 are +5V_LED; pins 6 and 10 are GND. Pin 8 is +5 V, matching
  the reference image's stated backlight pin. No 1 A load is expected: 20
  nominal 5 mA LEDs draw about 100 mA total. Protect and size the host supply
  for the measured board load; the 1 A value is a supply capacity, not a
  required current.
